Methodology

Security Workflow

A disciplined, repeatable process for every assessment — from initial recon through documented remediation.

🔎
Phase 01 — Reconnaissance

Information Gathering

Passive and active enumeration of the target environment. Mapping services, technologies, open ports, potential misconfigurations, and entry vectors before touching the target.

📊
Phase 02 — Analysis

Vulnerability Assessment

Structured analysis of gathered data to identify and prioritize vulnerabilities. Cross-referencing CVEs, misconfigurations, and logic flaws against the discovered attack surface.

💥
Phase 03 — Exploitation

Controlled Attack Simulation

Executing targeted, controlled exploits in isolated lab environments to validate vulnerability severity. Documenting each step to establish a reproducible proof-of-concept.

📝
Phase 04 — Reporting & Remediation

Documentation & Fixes

Translating technical findings into structured reports with risk ratings, business impact analysis, and actionable remediation steps. Security is only complete when the fix is verified.
